# Contributing to this research fork

AI-assisted coding, instrumentation, experiments, tests, and research are allowed in `teamleaderleo/FEX`.

This repository is an owned experimental fork. Work created here is fork-local research and must not be represented as an upstream FEX contribution merely because it exists here.

Any future contribution to the upstream FEX project must be independently prepared in accordance with the upstream repository's current contribution policy at the time of submission.
