# Contributors

This owned fork is used for internal FEX research and experimentation.

Agents and other fork-local contributors may contribute experimental source changes, tests, workflows, fixtures, instrumentation, and documentation when relevant to an investigation. The owner explicitly permits creating and launching GitHub Actions workflows, rerunning jobs, iterating on disposable CI branches, and inspecting retained logs and artifacts in owned repositories and forks.

Research commits and workflow commits in this fork are not upstream FEX submissions. Exact-revision experiments should record the product revision separately from workflow-only, policy-only, or harness-only fork commits.

The human owner decides what, if anything, is later prepared for upstream review. Any upstream candidate must be prepared separately under the upstream project's then-current contribution requirements.

Avoid accidental third-party GitHub backlinks and do not contact, modify, or dispatch workflows in upstream FEX without explicit authorization.
